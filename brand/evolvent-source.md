# EvolventAI identity assets

The company wordmark is the original inline vector mark displayed in the navigation of [evolvent.co](https://evolvent.co/en). `src/evolvent-wordmark.svg` preserves its path geometry, adds an accessible title, and uses the company's dark ink color.

The company symbol in `src/evolvent-symbol.svg` is the official [favicon SVG](https://evolvent.co/favicon.svg), with its original geometry and color. It is displayed beside the wordmark in the footer. The homepage also includes the company wordmark in its project attribution.

These are company identity assets, separate from ELE's original Earth mark. Use them to identify EvolventAI as the project's initiator; they are not a license for unrelated uses of the brand.
